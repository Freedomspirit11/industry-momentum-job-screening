# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-30

- Published a reusable industry-to-role career screening research workflow.
- Added city priority based on comparable *subsector supply-chain scale*, then completeness and verified job opportunities.
- Introduced company hiring classifications A–E and job-level education screening evidence levels H0–H4.
- Added evidence logs, counter-evidence searches, and output schemas.
- Generalized the source document to remove an individual jobseeker's private profile and preferences.
